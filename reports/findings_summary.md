# Findings Summary (Sprint 2)

**8,177,541 complaints** in the window. **Credit reporting** alone is 88% of them.

## What stands out

1. **The biggest theme is "bank, money, check, funds"**, covering 21% of written complaints.
2. **Money transfer complaints are the angriest** (average sentiment -0.54, vs -0.14 overall).
3. **Fastest-growing issue: Debt collection, "Took or threatened to take negative or legal action"**, up 168% (2999 to 8024 complaints a month).

## Caveats

Sentiment scores come from a general-purpose lexicon, so they track tone, not whether the complaint is valid. Complaint volume reflects who files with the CFPB, not every unhappy customer.
